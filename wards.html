            WELCOME TO WARDS

            Chart vitals at the bedside, faster

            One department-aware charting tool for General, ICU, Surgery, Pediatrics and Maternity patients. A detailed tour of everything inside — feel free to skip ahead any time.

            PATIENTS

            Add a patient, then log readings

            Tap + Add patient on the left: name/initials, bed, optional MRN, and a department. Each department unlocks its own extra fields — vent settings and RASS sedation for ICU, a POD counter and wound output for Surgery, weight-based dosing and growth for Pediatrics, and the full maternity/neonatal fields for Maternity.

            VITALS & NEWS2

            Every reading scores itself

            HR, BP, RR, SpO₂, temperature, GCS and fluids all log to one running table per patient. NEWS2 is calculated the moment you save a reading, and a sudden worsening trend triggers an on-screen alert — no separate calculator needed.

            SAFETY

            Problems, escalation, isolation, allergies

            Keep a running problem list, log who you escalated to (senior/registrar/consultant), and set isolation precautions and known allergies — both show as an always-visible banner on that patient's chart so nobody misses them.

            BOARD · BED MAP · ARCHIVE · CENSUS

            See the whole ward at a glance

            Switch views from the tray above the patient list: Board view for everyone's latest vitals in one table, Bed map for a floor-plan layout, Archive for discharged patients, and Ward census for the auto-logged daily male/female/neonatal count.

            SHIFT TOOLS & MORE…

            Less-common actions, tucked away

            The sidebar's Shift tools… holds SBAR handover for all your patients at once, an end-of-shift summary, CSV/JSON import and roster sorting. On a patient's own page, More… holds nickname, ID QR, baselines, rounding checklist, patient comparison, timeline, print/export and discharge — everyday actions stay up front, everything else is one tap away.

            OPTIONAL CLOUD SYNC

            Bring your team in when you're ready

            Sign in from the header, create or join a hospital, optionally group by department, then create a ward group. Patients in that group sync live to everyone on it — invite teammates by email, no console or IT ticket needed.

            YOUR DATA

            Local by default, sync if you choose

            Everything you chart stays on this device unless you sign in and select a hospital and ward group — then it syncs in real time with your team. Nothing leaves your device otherwise.
